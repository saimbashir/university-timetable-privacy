Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 3, 2026

Thank you for using University Timetable Pro. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles your information.

Information We Collect

University Timetable Pro does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.

Local Storage

Your timetable, attendance records, streak progress, reminder settings, and IQ game progress are stored only on your own device using SQLite local storage. No data is uploaded to our servers.

Notifications

The application uses local notifications only to remind you about scheduled classes and streak activities. Notifications are generated entirely on your device.

Internet Connection

The application does not require an internet connection for its core features.

Advertising

The current version of University Timetable Pro does not display advertisements.

Data Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties because no personal information is collected.

Children's Privacy

The application is intended primarily for students aged 16 years and above. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Any updates will be published on this page.
