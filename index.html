Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

Thank you for using University Timetable Pro. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how University Timetable Pro handles information and data when you use the application.

Information We Collect

University Timetable Pro does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

The application does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, camera data, or microphone recordings.

Local Storage

Information created while using University Timetable Pro, including timetable information, attendance records, streak progress, reminder settings, and IQ game progress, is stored locally on the user's device.

The application uses local device storage, including SQLite, to provide these features. This information is not uploaded to our servers.

Notifications

University Timetable Pro uses local notifications to remind users about scheduled classes and streak-related activities.

Notification scheduling and reminder information are handled locally on the user's device. The application does not send users' timetable or reminder information to our servers.

Users can control notification permission through their device settings.

Internet Connection

An internet connection is not required for the application's core timetable, attendance, reminder, and game functionality.

The developer does not operate a server that receives users' timetable, attendance, reminder, or IQ game information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share users' personal information with third parties because University Timetable Pro does not transmit this information to the developer's servers.

Advertising

The current version of University Timetable Pro does not display advertisements.

Data Security

University Timetable Pro is designed to keep user-created timetable and application data locally on the user's device.

Because this information is stored locally and is not transmitted to our servers, users should protect access to their device using appropriate device security features.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored application data remains on the user's device while the application and its associated local data remain on the device.

Users can remove locally stored application data by clearing University Timetable Pro's app data through Android settings or by uninstalling the application.

Because University Timetable Pro does not maintain user accounts or store this application data on developer-operated servers, we do not retain a server-side copy after it is removed from the user's device.

Children's Privacy

University Timetable Pro is intended primarily for students aged 16 years and above.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this privacy policy page, and the "Last Updated" date will be revised accordingly.
