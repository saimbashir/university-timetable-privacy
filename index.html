Privacy Policy

University Timetable Pro · Last updated: September 19, 2026

The core timetable and study features work offline. In ad-supported versions, Google AdMob may use internet access and process advertising-related data as described below.

Overview

University Timetable Pro is a timetable, attendance, reminders, and study/game app. This policy explains how the app handles user information, including when its advertising features are enabled. The core study features work offline, and the developer does not operate a server that receives your timetable, attendance, reminders, or game progress.

Information you enter and local storage

Timetables, attendance records, streak progress, reminder settings, and game progress are stored on your device, including through local storage such as SQLite. The app does not require an account or ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, camera data, or microphone recordings to use its core features.

The app's core features do not upload this user-created study information to developer-operated servers. Advertising data practices are described separately below; the fact that your timetable works offline does not mean the advertising SDK never sends information over the internet.

Advertising with Google AdMob

Ad-supported versions of University Timetable Pro use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements, including banner, native, and app-open ads. Ad requests in the current planned integration are configured to request non-personalized ads. Non-personalized advertising can still involve data processing for delivering ads, frequency capping, aggregated reporting, and fraud prevention.

When advertising is enabled and the SDK is used, Google may automatically collect and share information such as your device's IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location), interactions with the app or ads (for example, launches, taps, and ad views), diagnostic information about performance, and device or account identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, where available). This information is processed by Google and relevant advertising partners for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention according to their policies and applicable consent choices.

Read Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . For Google's description of information collected by the Mobile Ads SDK, see: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure .

Consent and advertising choices

Where required by applicable law and Google policies, the app should request advertising-related consent before requesting ads. Users may be presented with Google's consent message depending on region and the configuration of the app. You can also manage or reset your Android advertising ID in your device settings, subject to your Android version.

A privacy policy alone does not implement a consent prompt or a way to change consent choices. Where required, the released app must provide the appropriate consent flow and access to privacy options. If you have a privacy question or request, contact the developer using the address below.

Internet connection and offline use

The core timetable, attendance, local reminders, and game functionality do not require an internet connection. Loading advertisements and consent-related messages may require internet access. When offline, ads may not be available; core functionality is intended to remain usable.

Notifications

The app uses local notifications to remind you about classes and streak-related activities. Reminder details and notification scheduling are handled on the device rather than being uploaded to developer-operated servers. You can manage notification permission in Android settings.

Data sharing and security

The developer does not sell your user-created timetable, attendance, reminders, or game information. The app's core features do not send that information to developer-operated servers. When ads are enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes and shares the advertising-related information described above with Google and applicable advertising partners.

User-created study data is stored on your device, so protecting access to your device helps protect it. Google states that data the Mobile Ads SDK transmits is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored study data remains on your device while the app and its data remain installed. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. The developer does not maintain a server-side copy of that locally stored information.

Google controls retention and deletion of data it processes for advertising under its own policies and available privacy controls. Consult Google's privacy policy and your device's advertising settings for more information.

Children's privacy and intended audience

University Timetable Pro is described as intended primarily for students aged 16 and above. The app's target-age declarations in Google Play must match its actual intended audience. Where applicable, the app must apply the appropriate advertising and consent protections for minors and comply with Google Play's Families policies if children are included in its target audience.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy when the app, advertising features, or data practices change. Updates will be posted at this same URL with a revised Last updated date.
